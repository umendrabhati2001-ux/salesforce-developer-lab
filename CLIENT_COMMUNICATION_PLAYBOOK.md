# 💼 Umendra Bhati – Foreign Client Communication Playbook
**Confidential Quick-Reference Guide for Inbound & Outbound Client Negotiations**

---

## 🎯 Core Mindset: How Foreign Clients (US / UK / EU) Think
- **No Trivia Grilling**: US/UK clients **do not** ask textbook questions ("What is difference between trigger and workflow?"). They care about **delivery, reliability, and communication**.
- **Concise & Confident**: Always keep replies to **2–4 clear sentences**. Never write long, apologetic, or overly subservient paragraphs.
- **Peer-to-Peer**: Treat the client as a business peer, not a strict boss. You are an expert consultant solving their pipeline backlog.

---

```text

Happy to hop on a brief 15-minute call to discuss your pipeline needs. What time works best on your calendar this week?

Umendra Bhati
Salesforce Developer & Architect
```

## 🎙️ 15-Minute Call Cheat Sheet (What to Say on the Call)

### 1. "Tell me a bit about your background and recent work?"
> *"I specialize in enterprise Salesforce DX development, particularly complex Apex architectures, asynchronous queues, and external integrations. Recently, I built an enterprise FinTech architecture connecting Salesforce Cases with Stripe's REST API for automated dispute refunds, with 100% test coverage and zero Governor Limit bottlenecks. All of it is live on my monorepo and interactive studio."*

### 2. "How many hours can you commit per week?"
> *"I can reliably commit between 20 to 30 hours per week, with dedicated daily overlap during your team's core sprint hours."*

### 3. "What are your rate expectations?"
> *"My standard rate is $45/hr for contract engineering. For long-term or steady retained sprints, I can do $40/hr."*

### 4. "How do you handle contracting, timesheets, and invoicing?"
> *"Very straightforward. I can invoice bi-weekly or monthly via Wise, Deel, or direct bank wire. If your company prefers platform escrow, I can also set up an Upwork Direct Contract where you have full tracking and zero paperwork."*

## 🚫 What NEVER to Say (Red Flags to Avoid)
1. ❌ Never say: *"Sir, please give me a chance, I am ready to work at any rate."*  
   *(Cheap rates make clients think code quality is bad. $40/hr sounds professional and trustworthy).*
2. ❌ Never mention: Specific local tier-3 cities or regional details unless directly asked about country. Keep location as: **"India (Available for US/UK/Global Remote Overlap)"**.
3. ❌ Never panic if they mention a tool you haven't memorized:  
   Say: *"I've worked extensively with standard REST/JSON architectures and Salesforce DX; adapting to your specific CI/CD or middleware stack takes me less than a day."*
